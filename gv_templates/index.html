About & help

Image-ProHasher — group_viewer.py v{{ script_version }} · {{ date_modified }}

Review duplicate-image groups built by CLI_Image_Grouper.py. Each group says why it was formed. Verify hashes just that group to prove which files are byte-identical.

How to review

  1. Open a group. Tap images to select them (tap ⤢ to view full size).
  2. Use Keep ▾ to keep one by a rule and queue the rest for trash, or select images and press Trash / Move / Copy.
  3. Nothing happens to your files until you press Review and Commit. Trashed files can be restored.
